Privacy

How Audio Trimmer treats your files

A short explanation of what happens to your audio and video, and what doesn’t.

Your files are processed on your device.

Your audio never leaves this browser tab

Audio Trimmer decodes, previews, trims and exports in your browser. It doesn’t upload your files to a server, and there is no server that could keep them.

No other companies involved

The fonts and the MP3 encoder are bundled with the app and served from the same site. While you work, your browser doesn’t contact any third-party service.

Nothing is saved

No accounts, cookies, analytics or browser storage. Your audio lives in memory while the tab is open, and it’s gone when you close it or load another file.

Video files stay local too

When you add a video, its sound is pulled out on your device. If your browser can’t read the file directly, the video plays silently in the page while its audio is recorded. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Exports use a normal download

The trimmed clip is built in memory and saved through your browser’s usual download flow, as a WAV or MP3 file on your own device.

What the website host can see

Like any website, the host that serves this page may log standard connection details, such as your IP address and browser type, when your browser loads the app. That’s about delivering the page. It never includes your audio.

The GitHub, LinkedIn and portfolio links in the footer only take you to those sites when you click them.

Check it yourself

Open your browser’s developer tools (usually F12) and switch to the Network tab. Load a file, trim it and export it. You’ll see the app’s own files being fetched, and no request carrying your audio.
